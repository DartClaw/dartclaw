# Surface sweep: shell and chat

**Plan**: dev/bundle/docs/specs/0.22.1/plan.json
**Story-ID**: S12

## Feature Overview and Goal

**Intent**: The chrome on 100% of views and the product's flagship surface are still the audit's "flat and gray" — the rail's separators are browser-default 3-D bevels, the rail clips instead of scrolling, the chat thread floats 558px above its composer, and a bad URL ejects the user out of the application entirely.

**Expected Outcomes** (2-4 user- or business-observable success conditions, each `[OC<NN>]`-tagged; scenarios anchor to these via `[OC<NN>]`):

- [OC01] The persistent shell reads as one designed surface: nothing falls through to browser-default rendering, the rail scrolls when its content overflows, and no control paints on top of another.
- [OC02] The chat thread sits against its composer, its pre-stream state is the canon's composed "thinking" object, and scrolling up to re-read an earlier message survives a streaming turn.
- [OC03] Empty and error states on these surfaces carry the brand recipe, and a bad URL lands inside the app shell with navigation intact.
- [OC04] The mobile drawer is fully keyboard-operable — Escape dismisses it and focus cannot reach the page behind the scrim.

## Architecture Decision

**Approach**: fix every defect at its lowest owning layer, and where that layer is a canon *rule*, **consume rather than author**. The two canon-owned rules this story depends on (`.messages` anchoring, the missing `hr` reset) are S01's per the hoist manifest; `app.css` carries the app-owned rules; templates and controllers carry markup and behaviour. This story re-syncs nothing and leaves the drift check untouched. It defines the drawer's keyboard contract, consumes S16's settled topbar/page-title contract, and owns only the optional parent back-navigation extension and showcase demonstration.
**Why this over alternatives**: an app-side override of `.messages` or a local `hr` rule would fail `check_design_system_sync.sh` outright *and* violate the canon closure; a local re-tone would break the plan's single-owner rule for surface tokens. S12 executes alone in W8, after S08–S11 have settled the templates the restart-banner relocation touches, so the old concurrency deferral no longer applies. Deferring the drawer contract or S12-specific back-navigation/showcase work to S14 was rejected: S14 reconciles documentation against what shipped, while S16 already owns and records the title-ownership rule.

#### DEFECT FOUND AND FIXED — `inert` stranded the page after drawer navigation

Surfaced by the fresh-context review and reproduced live at 768px: tapping a chat row in the open drawer navigates by HTMX, which replaces `#sidebar` out-of-band with server markup carrying no `.open`. The drawer closed visually without ever going through `setSidebarOpen`, so TI13's `inert` boundary stayed on — with `.menu-toggle`, the only control that could lift it, *inside* that boundary. The entire right column (topbar, restart banner, main content, composer) was unclickable and unfocusable with no recovery short of a reload. Introduced by this story: before TI13 there was no `inert`, so the same class desync was cosmetic.

The first fix attempt was wrong and instrumentation caught it. Reconciling in `handleAfterSwap` had no effect because `#sidebar` still carries `.open` at *every* `oobAfterSwap` (9) and `afterSwap` (4) and only loses it by `afterSettle` (4). `reconcileDrawerState()` therefore runs from `handleAfterSettle`, re-deriving the boundary from the DOM rather than trusting that every close went through the setter. A `matchMedia('(max-width: 768px)')` change listener closes the sibling hole: an "open" drawer carried across a resize to desktop, where `.menu-toggle` is `display: none` and could never lift the region.

Verified live after the fix — drawer navigation leaves `.shell-main` and `.skip-link` non-inert with menu-toggle, composer and `#main-content` all focusable; reopening still contains focus; closing restores focus to `.menu-toggle`; a 768→1440 resize with the drawer open closes it and lifts `inert`. Pinned in `app_js_test.dart`, including an ordering assertion that the reconcile sits inside `handleAfterSettle`.
